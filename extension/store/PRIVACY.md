# Privacy Policy — Demoscope Recorder

_Last updated: 2026-07-22_

Demoscope Recorder ("the extension") is designed to run entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** The extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
information or usage data. There is no analytics and no tracking.

## What the extension accesses

To create a recording, the extension captures screenshots and a video of the
browser tab you choose to record, together with the interactions you perform on
that tab (clicks, typing, scrolling, and navigation). This content is processed
and stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own computer.

## Where your data goes

Nowhere. The extension has no backend server and no user accounts. Your
recordings never leave your device unless you explicitly export a file and share
it yourself.

## Third parties

The extension does not send data to any third party and contains no third-party
analytics, advertising, or tracking code.
